Common questions

Where is my shopping list stored?
Shopping items and app settings are stored locally on your device. If iCloud is available and enabled for the app, data may sync through your private iCloud account.

Why does the app ask for photo access?
Photo access is only needed if you choose a custom background image.

Can I bring back bought items?
Yes. Recently bought items can be shown and reactivated in the app.

Does the app require an account?
No. Shopping List does not require a separate account from the developer.
