# Privacy Policy for Shopping List

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Shopping List is an iOS app by Martin Lanius. This privacy policy explains what information the app uses and why.

## Information Used by the App

Shopping List stores shopping items, product names, quantities, bought status, and timestamps locally on your device. This information is used to show your shopping list and the list of recently bought products inside the app.

The app may also store settings such as theme mode, background color, element opacity, and an optional selected background image.

## Photo Library

When you choose a custom background image, Shopping List may request access to your photo library. The selected image is used as the app background and stored in the app settings.

Shopping List does not access photos unless you choose an image.

## iCloud Sync

Shopping List uses SwiftData and may use CloudKit for iCloud sync. If iCloud is available and enabled for the app, shopping items and app settings may sync through your private iCloud account.

Martin Lanius does not operate its own server for this data.

## Data Storage

Shopping items and app settings are stored locally on your device. If iCloud sync is active, this data may also be stored in your private iCloud account.

## Tracking and Advertising

Shopping List does not use third-party advertising SDKs.

Shopping List does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Shopping List does not sell your personal data.

## Data Sharing

Shopping List does not share shopping items, product names, app settings, or selected background images with data brokers or advertising networks.

iCloud sync is handled through Apple CloudKit as described above.
